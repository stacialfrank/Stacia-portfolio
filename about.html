GET TO KNOW ME

Technology with people at the center.

I’m Stacia Frank, a senior at the University of Oklahoma pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Science and Technology, with an expected graduation date of May 2027.

My interests sit at the intersection of technology, people, research, and creativity. Through coursework and hands-on experience, I’ve become especially interested in human-computer interaction, UX research, usability testing, accessibility, and the ways emerging technology can be designed around real human needs.

As a UX and Marketing Intern and Beta Testing Coordinator with ZenZiee, I’ve gained experience evaluating digital experiences, collecting and analyzing user feedback, identifying usability concerns, and communicating recommendations with a cross-functional team.

My background also includes STEM education, technical projects, campus involvement, and leadership. Whether I’m helping students build and code robots, testing a digital product, or developing a technical project, I’m most energized by work that makes technology more useful, understandable, and meaningful.

After graduation, I plan to pursue a master’s degree focused on human-computer interaction, UX, or human-centered technology. My goal is to build a career where I can combine research, design, technology, and leadership to improve the experiences people have with digital products.
